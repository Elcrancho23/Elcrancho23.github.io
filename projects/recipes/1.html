Portuguese Bifana Recipe
Bifana or Portuguese Pork Sandwich
  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 20 minutes
  • Servings: 4 sandwiches
  • Tips: Use Portuguese paprika for more flavor.
  • Marinate the pork overnight if you have time.
  • Serve on fresh crusty rolls.
Ingredient Amount Notes
Pork loin (thinly sliced) 500g (1 lb) Or pork shoulder
Garlic 3 cloves Minced
Paprika 2 tsp Portuguese if possible
White wine 1/2 cup Dry
Bay leaf 1 Optional
Olive oil 2 tbsp
Salt and pepper To taste
Crusty Portuguese rolls 4 Or any crusty sandwich bun
Spicy mustard To serve Optional
  • Instructions:
  • Mix garlic, paprika, salt, pepper, and white wine in a bowl. Add pork slices and marinate for at least 30 minutes, overnight is best.
  • Heat olive oil in a large pan. Add the pork and cook until browned and cooked through (about 5-6 minutes per side).
  • Add the marinade and bay leaf to the pan. Simmer for a few minutes until slightly reduced.
  • Warm the bread rolls. Fill each roll with pork and spoon some sauce on top.
  • Serve hot, with spicy mustard.
