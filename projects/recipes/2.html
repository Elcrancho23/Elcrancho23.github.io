Recipe for Alfajores

What are Alfajores? Sweet sandwich cookies filled with creamy dulce de leche, popular in South America.

  • Tip: Chill dough before baking for easier handling.
  • Tip: Roll dough gently for soft, tender cookies.
  • Tip: Dust finished cookies with powdered sugar for a classic look.
Ingredient Amount Notes
Butter 1 cup Room temperature
Sugar 2/3 cup
Egg yolks 2
Flour 2 cups All purpose
Cornstarch 1 cup
Baking powder 1 tsp
Dulce de leche 1 cup For filling
Powdered sugar For dusting Optional
  1. Step 1: Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add egg yolks and mix well.
  2. Step 2: Sift together flour, cornstarch, and baking powder. Gradually add to butter mixture until a soft dough forms.
  3. Step 3: Chill dough for 30 minutes. Roll out to 1/4 inch thick, cut into rounds.
  4. Step 4: Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10 minutes, until just set but not browned. Cool.
  5. Step 5: Spread dulce de leche on half the cookies, top with remaining cookies to make sandwiches.
  6. Step 6: Dust with powdered sugar if desired.
  • Prep time: 30 min
  • Cook time: 10 min
  • Servings: 12 cookies
